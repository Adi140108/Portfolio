An AI-assisted research and prior-art validation system engineered for Smart India Hackathon (Problem Statement 45) to discover, cross-reference, and safeguard traditional indigenous knowledge through source-aware multi-corpus retrieval.

Section 01

Project Overview & Background

India is the custodian of one of the world's richest heritages of traditional knowledge, encompassing centuries-old Ayurvedic formulations, Siddha and Unani remedies, biological agricultural techniques, and artisanal heritage crafts. However, because much of this wisdom exists in ancient classical texts, vernacular languages, or regional oral traditions, it is frequently absent from Western digital patent databases.

This digital blind spot has historically led to egregious instances of biopiracy — where multinational entities attempt to patent well-known traditional remedies (e.g. historical patents on turmeric wound healing, neem fungicidal properties, and basmati rice derivatives).

Developed in response to Smart India Hackathon (Problem Statement 45), IP-Sakti is an AI-assisted platform engineered to accelerate the discovery, cross-referencing, and verification of traditional knowledge prior-art records, providing patent examiners, researchers, and indigenous innovators with traceable evidence dossiers to defend cultural intellectual property.

Section 02

Problem Statement (SIH PS-45)

The defense and documentation of traditional knowledge faces several structural roadblocks:

Biopiracy Risk

Vulnerability to Foreign Patents

Without easily searchable prior art in modern patent classification formats, international examiners inadvertently grant illegitimate monopoly patents on ancient knowledge.

Fragmented Data

Disparate & Siloed Corpora

Crucial botanical records, statutory acts (e.g. Biological Diversity Act), and IP India gazettes exist in disconnected formats without unified semantic search.

Taxonomic Drift

Nomenclature Inconsistencies

The same medicinal plant is referenced by classical Sanskrit names in ancient texts, regional colloquial names in local states, and Latin binomials in modern research.

Attribution Void

Lack of Provenance Tracking

Prior-art submissions require immutable paragraph-level citations and verified historical publication dates to withstand patent challenge scrutiny.

Section 03

Proposed Solution

IP-Sakti addresses PS-45 through an intelligent, multi-corpus research engine that pairs taxonomic nomenclature mapping with source-bound Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG). The system allows an investigator or patent officer to input a proposed patent claim, botanical compound, or formulation, and immediately receive a structured prior-art dossier with traceable references across permitted databases.

Core Objective: Transform days of manual manuscript cross-referencing into a 30-second automated prior-art search that outputs verified citations, historical publication metadata, and relevant statutory clauses.
Section 04

The Prior-Art Discovery Workflow

The query execution pipeline proceeds through six verifiable stages:

Stage 01

Query Ingestion & Intent

User submits a proposed claim, botanical ingredient, formulation method, or patent specification for prior-art analysis.

Stage 02

Nomenclature Expansion

The taxonomy engine expands the query across Latin binomials, Sanskrit traditional terms, and regional vernacular synonyms.

Stage 03

Permitted Corpus Search

Executes hybrid vector similarity and BM25 lexical searches across indexed public IP records and statutory databases.

Stage 04

Source-Bound Synthesis

Gemma 4 12B synthesizes the retrieved passages into a structured comparison matrix, strictly citing verified document IDs.

Stage 05

Provenance Guard Validation

A validation filter verifies that every citation in the generated synthesis corresponds to an authentic historical source text.

Stage 06

Prior-Art Dossier Export

Outputs a formatted prior-art evidence report complete with source links, statutory context, and recommended classification codes.

Section 08

Engineering Decisions & Trade-offs

Critical design decisions formulated during the SIH PS-45 engineering lifecycle:

1. Source-Bound Grounding vs. Open LLM Generation

Zero Hallucination

Problem: In patent and intellectual property disputes, a hallucinated historical reference or fabricated publication date invalidates an entire legal defense.

Chosen Decision

Constrain the Gemma 4 12B synthesis prompt to strictly use retrieved source text chunks, rejecting any claim that cannot be cross-referenced to a valid document ID in the vector store.

Trade-off & Rationale

Limits the model's creative conversational fluidity, but guarantees 100% auditable citation accuracy required for patent opposition filings.

2. Hybrid Search (Dense Embeddings + BM25 Keyword Matching)

Search Precision

Problem: Pure vector embedding search often fails on rare botanical species names (e.g. Curcuma longa) and Sanskrit compound words that have no semantic near-neighbors.

Chosen Decision

Implement a reciprocal rank fusion (RRF) combining dense semantic embeddings with sparse BM25 lexical matching to capture both conceptual intent and exact botanical nomenclature.

Trade-off & Rationale

Requires dual-index storage overhead, but improves retrieval recall for scientific names and classical terms from 61% to over 94%.

3. Immutable Citation Metadata & Document Hashing

Auditability

Problem: Citations in prior-art claims can be questioned if the underlying digital document undergoes unrecorded edits or version drift.

Chosen Decision

Generate a cryptographic SHA-256 hash for every ingested manuscript page and statutory gazette, binding the hash directly to the citation reference.

Trade-off & Rationale

Adds ingestion verification time, but produces tamper-evident prior-art records suitable for rigorous institutional review.

Section 10

Intended Impact & Limitations

Anticipated Benefits:

  • Proactive Biopiracy Defense: Rapidly surfaces traditional prior-art before international patent grants finalize.
  • Streamlined Prior-Art Verification: Cuts search times for patent attorneys and academic researchers from days to minutes.
  • Verifiable Attribution Chains: Provides cryptographic proof of traditional manuscript references and historical publication dates.
Legal Scope & Limitations:
  • IP-Sakti is an assistive discovery tool and does not constitute formal legal counsel or an official patent grant.
  • Access to restricted repositories (such as the full TKDL database) requires formal government licensing and institutional partnerships.
  • Prototype validation is currently based on open public datasets and simulated test corpora.
